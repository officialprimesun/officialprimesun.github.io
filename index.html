01 / WHO WE ARETHE PRIMESUN STORY

A BIGGER PICTURE

Not just a name.
A vision in motion.

PRIMESUN is an emerging multi-brand business identity founded in 2026 by Nawazish Turk.

Our journey begins with consumer-focused home-care products. The ambition is to build a diverse portfolio over time, entering new categories with the same focus on practical products, quality-led thinking and accessible pricing.

We believe lasting businesses are built step by step — through thoughtful development, responsible decisions and trust earned over time.
