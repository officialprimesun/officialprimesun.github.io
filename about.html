PRIMESUN began in 2026 with a long-term ambition: build a growing family of brands, starting with practical consumer products and expanding thoughtfully over time.

01 / OUR STORYTHE FOUNDATION

A business identity built around a bigger vision.

PRIMESUN is an emerging multi-brand business identity operated by Nawazish Turk. It is not being presented as an incorporated company. The business is currently in its early stage, operating from the Roorkee area, with a focus on home-care products through COLIZON and FreshSoul.

The ambition is to build a diverse business portfolio over time, with a focus on quality-led product development, accessible pricing and dependable delivery.
